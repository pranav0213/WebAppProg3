How to Make Chai

  1. Boil water with tea leaves and spices
    1. Add 1 cup water and 2 tsp tea leaves to a pan
    2. Add crushed cardamom and ginger
  2. Add milk and sugar, then simmer
  3. Strain into a cup and serve hot
